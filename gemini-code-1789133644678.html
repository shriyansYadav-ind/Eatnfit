💡 THIS MONTH'S NUTRITION FOCUS

Healthy eating is not about eating less—it is about eating better. A balanced Indian diet can include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, adequate protein, healthy fats, and sufficient water. Small, consistent changes can support healthier long-term habits.

📋 5 SIMPLE TIPS FOR BETTER NUTRITION

  1. Start your day well: Choose a nutritious breakfast.
  2. Add vegetables: Include vegetables in lunch and dinner.
  3. Choose better grains: Try atta, oats, jowar, bajra, or brown rice.
  4. Include protein: Add a suitable protein source to each major meal.
  5. Stay hydrated: Drink adequate fluids through the day.

🥑 FOOD OF THE MONTH

Guava

A convenient whole fruit that provides fibre and vitamin C. Enjoy it as a whole fruit rather than as juice.

🍲 EASY INDIAN MEAL IDEA

Vegetable Moong Dal Khichdi + Curd + Salad

A simple, comforting meal that can be adapted to individual nutritional requirements.

❌ NUTRITION MYTH

Myth: "Carbohydrates should always be avoided for weight management."

Fact: The type, portion, and overall dietary pattern matter. Nutritious carbohydrate sources can be part of a balanced diet.

❤️ REMEMBER

Don't aim for a perfect diet. Aim for sustainable healthy habits.
